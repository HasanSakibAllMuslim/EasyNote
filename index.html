User manual

Set up for teaching

  1. Open EasyNote in Chrome or EdgeThese give the smoothest pen and the steadiest full screen.
  2. Tap Full screenIt's at the top right of My notes, or press F. Opening a note also switches it on.
  3. It stays full screenEsc won't throw you out. It only turns off when you tap the full-screen button again.
  4. Optional: install it as an appClick the install icon in the address bar to open EasyNote from your desktop or taskbar, always full screen.
Smartboard tip: use the pen or your finger to write. To move the page, choose the hand tool, hold Space, or use two fingers.

Install on iPad (best full screen)

  1. Open EasyNote in SafariOther browsers on iPad can't add it to the Home Screen.
  2. Tap the Share buttonThe square with an arrow pointing up, at the top of Safari.
  3. Tap Add to Home ScreenThen tap Add.
  4. Open EasyNote from your Home ScreenIt opens full screen every time, with no ✕ button, and it never drops out.
Apple Pencil: once you've used the Pencil, your palm and fingers no longer draw. One finger moves the page; two fingers pinch to zoom.

What you can do

  • ☁️ Real-time cloud saveEvery stroke is saved on your device straight away and to the cloud seconds later. If the computer shuts down, your work is safe.
  • 📄 Open and mark up PDFsWrite, highlight and draw on any PDF, then download it with your notes on it.
  • ✒️ PensPen, pencil, marker, brush and dashed pens, in any colour and size. Your pen settings are remembered.
  • 📐 Shape assistRough lines turn straight, and rough circles, rectangles and triangles turn into clean shapes.
  • 🖍️ Smart highlighterSwipe across PDF text for a neat highlight that snaps to the line.
  • 🔴 Laser pointerA glowing red trail that fades away, for pointing while you explain.
  • 🧩 Ready-made shapesLines, arrows, polygons, tables, XY axes, graph paper, number lines, 3D shapes, logic gates, truth tables and circuit symbols.
  • ✋ Select, move and resizeChange the colour or thickness, duplicate, delete, or bring to front.
  • 🗂️ PagesSee all pages at a glance, and add, reorder or delete them. Backgrounds can be black, white, grid, lined, dark grid or green board.
  • ⬇️ Download PDFDownload any note as a PDF, from inside the note or from My notes.
